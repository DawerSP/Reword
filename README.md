## 🚀 About Reword (Coming Soon)

Say goodbye to endless tab-switching and broken concentration. **Reword** is an upcoming browser extension that puts a powerful AI assistant directly at your fingertips, exactly where you work, read, and create. 

*We are currently building this project from the ground up. Stay tuned for our first release!*

---

## ✨ Planned Features

- **🧠 Smart Paraphrasing:** Instantly rephrase any text to sound more professional, human, or concise with just one click.
- **🎯 Tailored Text Generation:** Choose your desired category and tone, and let the AI draft exactly what you need.
- **⚡ Instant Summaries:** Get accurate TL;DRs of lengthy articles or documents in seconds.
- **✉️ One-Click Email Replies:** Draft context-aware, perfect responses to emails and messages directly within your inbox.

---

## 🛠️ Built With (Tech Stack)

We are crafting this extension using modern and reliable technologies:
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript (Chrome Manifest V3)
- **Backend & AI:** Node.js connected to state-of-the-art AI Models
- **Payments & Auth:** Stripe & Supabase
